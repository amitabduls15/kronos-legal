Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kronos Discord bot collects, uses, and protects information when you use it. By using the Bot, you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Bot, we may collect the following:

DataPurpose
User ID (Discord snowflake)Identifying users for moderation, settings, and features
Server (Guild) IDStoring per-server configuration
Channel IDDelivering messages and features to the correct channel
Message content (when commanded)Processing commands and moderation actions
TimestampsLogging and rate limiting

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, or payment information. We do not read or store private direct messages unless you explicitly invoke the Bot in them.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Bot's features.
  • To enforce moderation rules and server settings.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and misuse.
  • To improve and maintain the Bot.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Data is stored on secure servers. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service or as required by law. Server administrators may request deletion of their server's data at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only:

  • With service providers necessary to operate the Bot (e.g., hosting).
  • When required by law or valid legal process.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Kronos, users, or the public.

5. Discord's Role

The Bot operates on Discord's platform. Discord collects and processes data according to its own Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for Discord's data practices.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. To exercise these rights, contact the bot owner via the support server. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us for removal.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact the bot owner via the support server or the contact method on the bot's profile.
